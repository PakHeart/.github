## About PakHeart

**PakHeart** is a NUST Living Lab research initiative focused on establishing
the foundations for a standardized, clinically credible and AI-ready
electrocardiogram (**ECG**) research resource for Pakistan.

The initiative brings together expertise across **health sciences, biomedical
signal processing, artificial intelligence, data engineering and research
governance** to support population-relevant cardiovascular research.

PakHeart is being developed as a research infrastructure initiative, with an
emphasis on standardization, data quality, clinical relevance, reproducibility
and responsible research practices.

---

## Why PakHeart?

Cardiovascular disease represents a major health challenge in Pakistan, while
modern ECG and cardiovascular AI research increasingly depends on large,
well-structured biomedical datasets.

Established international ECG datasets provide important resources for
developing and benchmarking computational methods. However, population-specific
research resources are also important for investigating differences across
populations, clinical environments and ECG acquisition systems.

**PakHeart aims to help address this gap by establishing the research
infrastructure required for population-relevant ECG research in Pakistan.**

The objective extends beyond collecting ECG recordings. PakHeart seeks to
establish a research resource that is:

| Principle | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Standardized** | Consistent representation of ECG signals and associated metadata |
| **Traceable** | Preservation of research provenance across the data lifecycle |
| **Quality Controlled** | Systematic assessment of data and signal quality |
| **Clinically Informed** | Clinical expertise incorporated into validation and interpretation |
| **Reproducible** | Research workflows designed to support transparent scientific evaluation |
| **Responsibly Governed** | Appropriate consideration of privacy, ethics and data stewardship |
| **AI-Ready** | Structured research data suitable for rigorous computational research |

## What We Are Building

PakHeart focuses on the core research infrastructure required to transform
ECG data into a reliable resource for scientific investigation.

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **ECG Standards & Metadata** | Establish consistent structures for ECG signals and associated research information |
| **Data Governance** | Support responsible handling, traceability and stewardship of research data |
| **Data Standardization** | Enable heterogeneous ECG sources to be represented consistently for research |
| **Quality Assurance** | Establish systematic approaches to assessing ECG data quality |
| **Clinical Validation** | Incorporate clinical expertise into annotation and validation processes |
| **Research Readiness** | Prepare validated ECG resources for biomedical and AI research |

## Responsible Research

PakHeart recognizes that biomedical data research requires appropriate
governance, privacy protection and responsible data stewardship.

The initiative is being developed around principles including:

- protection of participant and patient privacy
- appropriate governance and access control
- data provenance and traceability
- reproducible research practices
- clinically informed validation
- responsible use of biomedical data 
- controlled management of research outputs.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Patient-identifiable information, restricted clinical records, credentials,
> consent records containing participant information, and other sensitive
> clinical information are not intended for storage in public PakHeart GitHub
> repositories.**

Public GitHub resources are intended for approved research documentation,
software, schemas, publications and other materials suitable for public release.
