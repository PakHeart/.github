## 🔵 About PakHeart

**PakHeart** is a NUST Living Lab research initiative focused on establishing the foundations for a standardized, clinically credible and AI-ready electrocardiogram (**ECG**) research resource for Pakistan.

The initiative brings together expertise in **cardiovascular research, health sciences, biomedical signal processing, artificial intelligence, data engineering and research governance**.

PakHeart aims to develop the standards, technical infrastructure, quality-control processes and clinical validation framework required to support **reproducible and population-relevant cardiovascular research**.

> [!NOTE]
> PakHeart is being developed as a research infrastructure initiative. The project is establishing the foundations required for future ECG research and AI development rather than presenting a completed clinical AI system.

---

## 🟢 Why PakHeart?

Cardiovascular disease represents a major health challenge in Pakistan. At the same time, much of modern ECG and cardiovascular AI research depends on large datasets collected outside Pakistan.

International ECG resources such as **PTB-XL** and **MIMIC-IV** provide important benchmarks for cardiovascular research and machine-learning development. However, population-specific resources are also important for studying differences in patient populations, clinical environments, acquisition systems and model generalizability.

PakHeart seeks to address this research gap by establishing the infrastructure required for a **standardized and clinically meaningful ECG research resource representing the Pakistani population**.

The goal is not simply to collect ECG files.

The goal is to build a research resource that is:

| | Principle | What it means |
|:---:|---|---|
| 🔵 | **Standardized** | ECG signals and metadata follow defined and consistent structures |
| 🟢 | **Traceable** | Data provenance can be maintained throughout the research lifecycle |
| 🔵 | **Quality Controlled** | File, signal and metadata quality can be systematically assessed |
| 🟢 | **Clinically Validated** | Clinical expertise contributes to annotation and validation |
| 🔵 | **Reproducible** | Research processing and analysis can be reconstructed and evaluated |
| 🟢 | **Responsibly Governed** | Privacy, ethics, access and data stewardship are considered from the outset |
| 🔵 | **AI-Ready** | Validated data can support rigorous machine-learning research |

## 🔵 Phase-0 — Building the Foundation

PakHeart is being developed through a phased research programme.

**Phase-0** represents the foundational stage of the initiative and focuses on establishing the infrastructure required for subsequent ECG research and scale-up.

The major areas of development include:

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Metadata & Standards** | Establishing standardized ECG metadata and data representations |
| 🔵 **Governance** | Developing responsible data-management and governance frameworks |
| 🟢 **Technical Infrastructure** | Establishing ECG ingestion, processing and standardization capabilities |
| 🔵 **Quality Control** | Developing methods for file, signal and metadata verification |
| 🟢 **Clinical Validation** | Establishing reproducible annotation and validation processes |
| 🔵 **Research Readiness** | Preparing the infrastructure required for future ECG and AI research |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Phase-0 is focused on **infrastructure, standardization and research readiness**. Future stages can build upon these foundations as the initiative develops.

## 🔵 ECG Standardization

ECG data can originate from different devices, software systems and file formats.

PakHeart therefore aims to establish a common research representation while maintaining the information necessary to understand the original source.

| Component | Standardization Focus |
|---|---|
| **ECG Waveform** | Consistent signal representation |
| **Lead Configuration** | Defined lead identification and organization |
| **Sampling Information** | Recording of relevant sampling characteristics |
| **Signal Units** | Consistent representation of ECG amplitude |
| **Acquisition Metadata** | Recording context and device-related information |
| **Clinical Metadata** | Structured clinically relevant information |
| **Quality Metadata** | Indicators supporting data-quality assessment |
| **Provenance** | Traceable history of source and processing |

---

## 🟢 Validation Framework

PakHeart considers validation at multiple levels.

| Validation Layer | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 🔵 **File Validation** | Verify that ECG source files can be correctly interpreted |
| 🟢 **Signal Validation** | Assess whether ECG signals are technically suitable for research |
| 🔵 **Metadata Validation** | Verify completeness and consistency of associated information |
| 🟢 **Annotation Validation** | Evaluate reliability of clinical labels and annotations |
| 🔵 **Pipeline Validation** | Confirm reproducibility of processing workflows |
| 🟢 **Research Validation** | Evaluate suitability for downstream scientific analysis |
| 🔵 **External Validation** | Study performance across populations, datasets or acquisition environments |

This layered approach helps distinguish **technical quality, clinical interpretation and AI performance** rather than treating them as a single validation problem.
